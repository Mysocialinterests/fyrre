## The observation

Information travels fast, but rarely well. Algorithms amplify anger. Scientific studies become weapons in debates. Knowledge stays confined to academic circles while the public navigates the noise alone.

## Public exhaustion

Algorithms reward anger, clickbait, and polarized opinions. The result? An exhausted, distrustful public, increasingly vulnerable to disinformation.

## Our approach

At Mes Intérêts Sociaux, we don't hand down absolute truths. We open the hood: we select rigorous public research, break down its methodology, its strengths and its limits — and translate it into concrete insights for everyday life.
